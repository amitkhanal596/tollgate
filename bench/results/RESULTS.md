## Failover

All times are on the Docker VM clock, offsets from the real T0 (no slack applied to the clock). **Failover time** = T0 to the client-visible end (request start + its latency) of the first failure cluster; `n/a` if no failure cluster began within 2 s of the fault. **Back in rotation** = restore until `demo-1` serves a 200 again.
